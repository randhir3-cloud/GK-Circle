### Domain Verification Status
* **Resend Dashboard**: `Pending` (awaiting SPF TXT record configuration).

### Issues Found & Required Action
> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Issue**: The SPF TXT record for the sending subdomain `mail.gkcircle.com` is missing. This prevents Resend from verifying the sending domain and causes external mail servers (Gmail/Outlook) to fail SPF alignment checks.
> 
> **Action Required**: Add the following record in the Cloudflare DNS dashboard for `gkcircle.com`:
> - **Type**: `TXT`
> - **Name**: `mail` (resolves to `mail.gkcircle.com`)
> - **Value**: `"v=spf1 include:feedback-smtp.us-east-1.amazonses.com ~all"`
> - **TTL**: `Auto` / `5 minutes`
> - **Expected Propagation**: ~5 minutes on Cloudflare.
